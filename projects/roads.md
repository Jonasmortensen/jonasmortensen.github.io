In 2025/2026 I worked at Raw Power Games on their ambitios sandbox war game Chronicles: Medieval. I was part of the procedural generation team more specifically working on terrain and settlement generation. This included buidling a suite of Road tools for artists and designers to control where roads go, and how they look. 

In the game the road network is generated procedurally based on a tiered list of points. Designers can decide on a priority for each tier and associate mechanical data. Artists can use the road rendering tool to define what the road looks like
